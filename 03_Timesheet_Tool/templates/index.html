How it works — step by step

Step 1 · Scope
Projects covered
Only GISS and BACS Jira projects are searched. Tickets from other projects will not appear.
Step 2 · Window
Date range checked
Last 3 days of the previous month through the last day of the selected month — so a ticket that entered implementation on Jun 30 appears in the July timesheet.
Step 3 · Filter
Which tickets qualify
A ticket appears only if you were ever the assignee and it entered the implementation status during the window. Tickets you only commented on are excluded.
Step 4 · Start Date
When your work began
The date the ticket entered the implementation status — provided you were the author of that transition, the assignee at that moment, or it was already in that status when you were assigned.
Step 5 · End Date
When your work ended
The date the ticket left the implementation status, or the date you were reassigned away — whichever came first. If neither has happened yet, End Date shows In Progress.
Step 6 · Duration
Days calculation
End Date − Start Date + 1, capped to the selected month boundaries. Shows N/A when the ticket is still In Progress (no end date yet).
